METHODOLOGY

What counts

Regular-season records and all-play are built from ESPN matchup scores. Historical playoff records count winners-bracket games only. Continuous win/loss streaks carry through winners-bracket playoff games and across seasons; byes do not count. Historical lineup/bench snapshots are not reliable enough for bench-efficiency claims, so they are intentionally excluded here.
