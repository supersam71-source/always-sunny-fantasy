How to read this

Methodology

2026 uses completed/live Week 11 totals from the fresh ESPN export. All-play asks what your record would be if you played all nine opponents each week. The Playoff Lab enumerates every remaining W/L branch and uses current PF only as a provisional tiebreak, so its percentages are outcome-tree shares rather than predictive odds. Historical starter decisions are not treated as reliable when the ESPN archive only exposes final-roster lineup slots.
